software legitimately obtained by its user, for the purposes of:

- **interoperability** — understanding file and protocol formats so
  independently created software (such as the
  [Serviimo](https://github.com/CyrixJD115/Serviimo) private server and
  [AniiModLoader](https://github.com/CyrixJD115/AniiModLoader)) can work with
  the user's own copy, the purpose expressly contemplated by 17 U.S.C.
  § 1201(f) in the United States and by Arts. 5–6 of EU Directive 2009/24/EC

- **personal study, education, and security research** (see 17 U.S.C. § 107
  and § 1201(g)).

This project is not a piracy tool. Where analysis touches technical measures

protected works. Reverse-engineering rights vary by jurisdiction — **users
are responsible for ensuring their use is lawful where they live.** If your
